On a touchscreen, use the left pad to move or aim. Hold Fire to keep firing, hold and release Charge for a charged shot, and tap Use to interact. Hold Up to aim high or to climb striped steel with magnetic wheels. On foot indoors, hold Fire to strafe while aiming in one direction.

Starting routes

Down to the pumps

From Service Dock, jump the first block and drop down the marked shaft. Park beside the cyan hatch in Flooded Approach, exit the rover and enter on foot.

Up to Magnetic Rise

Install the magnetic wheels at the Pump Control console and return to the rover. Drive right through Drain Channel to Service Dock. Hold Up against the striped wall and take the upper-right exit to Magnetic Rise.

Crane Dock prototype

Lower the winch, fit the suspension coil, and jump to the powered sorter for a return ride to the pump. Enter Crane Dock. This standalone annex keeps its own save.

Into the sound works

For the optional route, stop at the marked gate before the first block at Service Dock. Press E or controller B in the rover. In Listening Cut, fire beneath the rail and watch what turns toward you.
